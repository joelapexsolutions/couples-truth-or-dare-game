Step by step

How to add a pack to the app

Download the pack first (tap Download on a pack below). It saves to your phone as a small file, usually in your Downloads folder. Then follow the steps for the game it belongs to.

Truth or Dare packs

  1. Open the Couples Games app and tap “Truth or Dare”You will find it on the Home screen under “More ways to play”.
  2. Tap “Settings”It is the button with the cog icon on the Truth or Dare start screen, next to Start Game and Reset.
  3. Scroll down to “Upload Custom Questions”It is below your question lists and the “Add New Question” button.
  4. Tap “Choose File”Your phone's file picker opens. Open your Downloads folder and tap the pack you downloaded.
  5. Check the pack details, then tap “Import Questions”You will see the pack name, description, question count and version first. Tap OK on “Question pack imported successfully!”.
  6. Go back and tap “Start Game”The imported pack is switched on automatically.

Good to know: while a pack is active, the game uses that pack's questions plus any questions you added yourself. To go back to the normal questions, open Settings, scroll to Active Question Pack and tap Return to Default Questions. Under Imported Question Packs you can tap Activate Pack to switch between packs, or the bin icon to remove one.

Couples Tasks packs

  1. Open the Couples Games app and tap “Couples Tasks”You will find it on the Home screen under “More ways to play”.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the pageGo past the Romantic Tasks, Passion Tasks and To Do Tasks lists, to the section called Upload Custom Tasks. There is no Settings button on this screen, the upload box is on the page itself.
  3. Tap “Choose File”Your phone's file picker opens. Open your Downloads folder and tap the tasks pack you downloaded.
  4. Check the pack details, then tap “Import Tasks”You will see the pack name, description, task count and version first. Tap OK on “Tasks pack imported successfully!”.
  5. Scroll back up and start ticking tasksThe imported pack is switched on automatically, and your Romantic and Passion lists now show its tasks.

Good to know: each tasks pack keeps its own progress, so switching packs will not lose your ticks. To go back to the normal tasks, scroll to Active Tasks Pack and tap Return to Default Tasks. Under Imported Tasks Packs you can tap Activate Pack to switch between packs, or the bin icon to remove one.

If the app says the pack format is invalid, download the file again from this page and retry. Don't have the app yet? Get it free on Google Play at the bottom of this page.
