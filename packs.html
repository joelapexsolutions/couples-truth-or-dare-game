Step by step

How to add a pack to the app

Download a pack below first. It saves to your phone as a small file, usually in your Downloads folder. Then pick your game and tap a step to open it.

Premium feature. Adding your own packs to Truth or Dare, and playing Couples Tasks, need Couples Games Premium. Packs you already have can always be switched on and off.
1Open Truth or Dare and tap Game settings

Open the Couples Games app and tap Truth or Dare under “More ways to play”. On the start screen, tap Game settings, the button under Start game.

2Tap Question packs

Under Your questions, tap Question packs. It also shows which pack you are playing with right now.

3Tap Add question packs and choose your file

In the Add your own box, tap Add question packs. Your phone's file picker opens. Go to your Downloads folder and tap the pack you downloaded. You can choose more than one file at a time.

4Check the pack, then tap Import this pack

The app reads the file and shows the pack's name, how many cards it has, and how they split across truths, dares and levels. If it looks right, tap Import this pack. Your other progress is kept safe, because each pack saves its own.

5Tap Start playing

You will see Pack added. The new pack is now your active pack, so tap Start playing to use it straight away.

6Switch back to your normal questions, or remove a pack

Open Game settings then Question packs at any time. Under Playing with, tap a pack to switch to it, including Default questions. Tap the bin icon on an imported pack to remove it. Questions you add yourself are always included.

1Open Couples Tasks and tap Task packs

Open the Couples Games app and tap Couples Tasks under “More ways to play”. Scroll down, past the wheel and your task list, and tap the Task packs tile next to the To-do list.

2Tap Add task packs and choose your file

In the Add your own box, tap Add task packs. Your phone's file picker opens. Go to your Downloads folder and tap the tasks pack you downloaded. You can choose more than one file at a time.

3Check the pack, then tap Import this pack

The app reads the file and shows the pack's name, how many tasks it has, and the split between Romantic and Passion tasks. If it looks right, tap Import this pack. Your other progress is kept safe, because each pack saves its own.

4Tap Start playing

You will see Pack added. The new pack is now your active pack, so tap Start playing to use it. Each pack keeps its own progress, hearts and to-do list.

5Switch back to your normal tasks, or remove a pack

Open Task packs at any time. Under Playing with, tap a pack to switch to it, including Default tasks. Tap the bin icon on an imported pack to remove it.

If the app says a file is not a valid pack, download it again from this page and retry. Only use packs from this page.
