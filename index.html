Questions

Good to know

What is Couples Games?

Couples Games is a free Android app for two. It has truth or dare for couples, a spin-the-wheel Couples Tasks game, Who's More Likely, a Long Distance mode and a daily tips section. It is made for new couples, long-term couples and married couples.

Is the app free?

Yes, you can download it free. The free version includes the Soft and Sensual levels of Truth or Dare (up to 10 rounds a game) and the first 10 questions of Who's More Likely. Premium is an optional subscription that removes ads and unlocks the Hot and Spicy levels, longer games, Couples Tasks, the Intimacy Profile and custom questions.

Is it only for people who are dating?

No. It works for a first few dates and for a twenty-year marriage. You pick the heat level that suits you both, and you can skip any card.

Can we play from different places?

Yes. One of you creates a Long Distance game in the app and shares the 6-character code. The other person opens the join page in any web browser, types the code and plays. Your partner can be on an iPhone or a laptop and does not need to install anything.

Is there an iPhone version?

Right now the app is on Android only. A partner on an iPhone can still join your Long Distance games from the browser.

How spicy does it get?

Truth or Dare has four heat levels: Soft, Sensual, Hot and Spicy. Soft and Sensual are free and flirty. Hot and Spicy are bolder and are part of Premium. The app is for adults, you choose the level together, and nobody has to do a card they do not want to.

What are question packs?

Packs are extra sets of questions and tasks you can download from this site and import into the app. Some are light and funny, some are made for new couples, and some are adults-only. See the packs page.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Choose Couples Games and tap Cancel subscription.
