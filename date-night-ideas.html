Date night ideas

50 date night ideas

For cosy nights in, for getting out of the house, and for when you are miles apart. Filter by mood, or let us decide.

  1. At home

    Cook a cuisine you have never tried

    Pick a country, shop for the ingredients together and cook it side by side.

  2. At home

    Build a blanket fort cinema

    Pillows, fairy lights, snacks and one film you both pick. Phones stay outside the fort.

  3. At home

    Hold a cocktail or mocktail tasting

    Make three recipes each, rate them out of ten and give the winner a ridiculous name.

  4. At home

    Recreate your first date

    Same food, same playlist, and the outfit if it still fits.

  5. At home

    Candlelit dinner, phones in a drawer

    Cook something nice, light the candles and put every screen out of reach.

  6. At home

    Slow dance in the living room

    Pick five songs from your first year together and dance to all of them.

  7. At home

    Swap massages

    Fifteen minutes each, good music, no talking about chores.

  8. At home

    Play a board game with stakes

    Loser does the dishes, or gives the winner a foot rub.

  9. At home

    Write each other love letters

    Ten minutes of writing, then read them out loud.

  10. At home

    Play Truth or Dare for couples

    Start on the Soft level in the Couples Games app and see where the night goes.

  11. Out and about

    Sunset picnic somewhere new

    Pick a spot neither of you has sat in before and bring something you can share.

  12. Out and about

    Take a class together

    Pottery, salsa or cooking. Being beginners together is half the fun.

  13. Out and about

    Farmers' market breakfast

    Wander the stalls, buy what looks good and cook it when you get home.

  14. Out and about

    Mini golf or bowling with a bet

    Loser buys dessert, or picks the next date.

  15. Out and about

    Walk a neighbourhood you never visit

    Choose a cafe at random and order whatever the other person picks.

  16. Out and about

    Catch live music in a small venue

    Go for a band neither of you knows.

  17. Out and about

    Outdoor cinema or drive-in

    Bring blankets and a flask of something warm.

  18. Out and about

    Museum date with one rule

    Each pick your favourite piece and explain why to the other.

  19. Out and about

    Ice cream shop crawl

    Three shops, one scoop each, then rank them.

  20. Out and about

    Stargazing away from the lights

    Drive somewhere dark, lie back and find five constellations.

  21. Adventure

    Hike to catch the sunrise

    Pack breakfast for the top and eat it with the view.

  22. Adventure

    Book a weekend away

    Choose somewhere under two hours away and plan nothing except where to eat.

  23. Adventure

    Rent kayaks or paddle boards

    Pack a snack and find a quiet bit of water.

  24. Adventure

    Try a climbing gym

    Take turns spotting and cheering each other on.

  25. Adventure

    Pick a direction and drive for an hour

    Stop at the first interesting thing you see.

  26. Adventure

    Camp out, even in the backyard

    Tent, fire or torches, and as many stars as the sky will give you.

  27. Adventure

    Learn something silly from a video

    Juggling, a card trick or a dance routine. Perform it for each other by the end of the night.

  28. Adventure

    Do an escape room

    Nothing shows how well you communicate like a locked door.

  29. Adventure

    Go go-karting

    Friendly rivalry, with bragging rights at stake.

  30. Adventure

    Run a photo challenge

    Ten prompts, ten photos, one hour. Vote on the best one.

  31. Romantic

    Run a bath for two

    Candles, music, and nobody checking their phone.

  32. Romantic

    Tell your how-we-met story

    Each of you tells it from your own side. Notice what is different.

  33. Romantic

    Make playlists for each other

    Ten songs each, with a sentence about why each one made the list.

  34. Romantic

    Breakfast for dinner, in bed

    Pancakes, candles and a film you have already seen ten times.

  35. Romantic

    Practise each other's love language

    Pick one each and put it into action for the whole evening.

  36. Romantic

    Write a five-year bucket list

    Dream big, then pick one thing from the list to start this month.

  37. Romantic

    Spend an hour of deep questions

    Take turns answering the Truth cards in the app, with the lights low.

  38. Romantic

    Give each other three yeses

    Everyone gets three requests the other will happily say yes to, within your comfort zones.

  39. Romantic

    Look through old photos

    Pick your five favourite photos of the two of you and tell the story behind each.

  40. Romantic

    Play the Sensual level together

    Dim the lights and draw a few cards. Stop whenever you like.

  41. Long distance

    Watch the same film on a video call

    Press play at the same moment and text your reactions.

  42. Long distance

    Cook the same recipe on a video call

    Chop, stir and then eat dinner together on screen.

  43. Long distance

    Play Long Distance Truth or Dare

    One of you starts a game in the app, the other joins from this website with a code.

  44. Long distance

    Send a date night box

    Post a little parcel with a snack, a candle and a note, to open together.

  45. Long distance

    Take a virtual museum tour

    Many museums let you wander their rooms online. Pick a favourite and compare notes.

  46. Long distance

    Read to each other

    A chapter of the same book every night.

  47. Long distance

    Have an online game night

    Pick a co-op game or an online board game and team up.

  48. Long distance

    Go for a walk and talk

    Walk at sunset while you chat, each in your own neighbourhood.

  49. Long distance

    Stargaze while you talk

    Both look at the same sky and pick out what you can see.

  50. Long distance

    Open a memory jar on a call

    Each write ten things you love about the other, then read them out loud.
